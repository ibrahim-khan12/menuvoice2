Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 26, 2026

Meet My Menu reads restaurant menus aloud and answers questions about them by voice. This page explains what information the app uses for that, why, and the choices available to you.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the Meet My Menu web app (app.meetmymenu.com) and the Meet My Menu AI iOS app, which is the same app packaged for the App Store. Both are referred to as "the app" below.

Information the app collects

Account information

Signing in with Google shares your email address and name with the app; Google verifies the identity token on the server, and the app never receives or stores your Google password. You can also use the app with a typed email address without Google Sign-In. Cloud sync is unavailable with email-only sign-in.

Profile and preferences

Allergies, foods you dislike, cuisines you like, past orders, a running history of restaurants you've asked about, and accessibility settings (theme, text size, speech rate, preferred voice). These exist so the app can warn about relevant allergens and tailor recommendations — they are not shared with anyone except as described below.

Saved restaurants and menus

When you save a restaurant, its name, menu contents, source, and location are stored so you can reopen it later without re-scanning or re-searching.

Menu photos

The camera is used to photograph menus so they can be read aloud. Menu images are sent to OpenAI for analysis (see below). The app does not keep a copy of an image as part of the normal scan-and-read flow.

Voice audio

The microphone is used for spoken questions and commands. Audio may be sent to OpenAI for transcription. Cartesia may receive text in order to generate spoken responses. Meet My Menu does not make a recording of microphone audio as part of that flow.

Usage information

The app logs screens used, actions taken (such as scanning a menu or saving a restaurant), timing, success or failure, and device details for debugging. Depending on the action, logs may include your email address, the text of a question, or the app's reply. Meet My Menu does not sell this information or use it for advertising.

Device information

App version and browser/device user agent are logged alongside usage information, for debugging.

How the information is used

  • To read menus aloud, answer questions about them, and warn about allergens you've told the app about.
  • To sync your profile and saved restaurants across devices, if you're signed in with Google.
  • To understand what's working and what isn't, so the app can be improved.

Meet My Menu does not run ads, does not use advertising trackers, and does not sell personal information.

Who information is shared with

The app relies on a small number of service providers to function. Each receives only what it needs to do its specific job:

  • OpenAI — processes menu photos, conversation questions, and voice transcription to generate the app's responses.
  • Cartesia — an alternate text-to-speech provider, used for some of the app's spoken voices.
  • Google — verifies your identity when you sign in with Google. The app only receives your verified email and name, not your Google password or account access.
  • Vercel — hosts the app and its backend storage (profile/restaurant sync, usage logs, and, if enabled, saved menu photos).
About allergen information: Meet My Menu surfaces ingredient and allergen information it can find so you know what to ask about — it is not a substitute for confirming directly with restaurant staff, and mistakes in source menus or in what the app understood are possible.

Camera and microphone access

The app only accesses the camera when you're actively scanning a menu, and the microphone only when you're actively asking a question or giving a voice command. Neither runs in the background.

Where data is stored

Your profile and saved restaurants are stored on your device first. If you're signed in with Google, that same data is also synced to Meet My Menu's cloud storage so it's available if you switch devices. All traffic between the app and its servers is encrypted (HTTPS).

How long data is kept

Local data stays on your device until you clear it or sign out. Cloud-synced profile data, saved restaurants, and usage logs are retained until you request deletion or the service's retention practices change.

Your choices

  • Use the app with a typed email instead of Google Sign-In when you do not want to use cloud sync.
  • Clear your saved restaurants and profile from Settings at any time.
  • Request a copy or deletion of your cloud-stored data by emailing 2firemaster27@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

Meet My Menu is not directed at children under 13, and the app does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests about your data, can be sent to 2firemaster27@gmail.com.
