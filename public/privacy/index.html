Meet My Menu Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Meet My Menu (“Meet My Menu,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is designed to help blind and visually impaired users independently access restaurant menus. The app can read menus aloud, answer questions about menu items by voice or text, surface ingredient and allergen information, and save restaurants and preferences for later use.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Meet My Menu collects, how that information is used, when it is shared with service providers, how long it is retained, and the choices you have regarding your information.

1. What This Privacy Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the Meet My Menu web application at app.meetmymenu.com, the Meet My Menu AI iOS application distributed through the Apple App Store, and related Meet My Menu services that link to this Privacy Policy. The web and iOS versions provide substantially the same Meet My Menu service and are referred to collectively as the “app.”

2. Information We Collect

The information Meet My Menu collects depends on how you use the app and which features you choose to use.

Account and Sign-In Information

If you choose Google Sign-In, Google provides basic account information needed to identify you, including your name and email address. Google handles authentication; Meet My Menu does not receive or store your Google password.

You may also be able to use Meet My Menu without Google Sign-In by entering an email address. In that case, information such as your profile and saved restaurants may remain stored locally on your device rather than syncing to an account. This does not mean no information leaves your device: menu images, questions, or voice input needed for AI features may still be sent securely to the providers described below.

Profile, Dietary, and Preference Information

You may provide food allergies, dietary restrictions or preferences, disliked foods, preferred cuisines, previous orders, restaurants viewed or asked about, and accessibility preferences including theme, text size, speech rate, and preferred voice. This optional information personalizes the app, improves accessibility, and helps identify menu items that may contain allergens you have told us about.

Saved Restaurants and Menu Information

If you save a restaurant or menu, we may store its name, location, menu contents, source, associated information, and menu images if you have specifically enabled menu-photo storage. This lets you reopen restaurants and menus without searching or scanning again.

Menu Photos and Camera Information

Meet My Menu can use your device camera to photograph physical menus. When you scan, the image is captured on your device, securely transmitted to an AI provider such as OpenAI for analysis, and extracted menu information is returned so it can be organized, displayed, read aloud, and used to answer questions.

Meet My Menu does not intentionally store the original menu photo in its own cloud storage unless you enable “Save menu photos for analysis.” That setting is off by default. If enabled, photos may be stored for troubleshooting, improving menu processing, and analyzing accuracy. Turning it off prevents future menu photos from being intentionally saved after processing. Providers processing images for us may maintain temporary processing or security logs under their agreements, policies, and legal obligations.

Voice, Conversations, and Usage Information

Your microphone is accessed only when you actively use a feature requiring voice input; Meet My Menu does not intentionally listen in the background. Audio may be securely transmitted to a provider such as OpenAI for transcription and understanding. Meet My Menu itself does not intentionally retain a permanent recording of microphone audio. Response text may be sent to Cartesia for spoken audio.

We may process and log questions, transcribed speech, commands, responses, menu items discussed, and related interaction information to provide the service, troubleshoot, understand use, and improve Meet My Menu. Signed-in usage may be associated with an account or email address. We do not use conversations for advertising.

We may collect screens or features used, actions and their outcome, timing, menu scanning and restaurant-saving activity, errors, app version, browser and device user-agent data, and general diagnostic/performance information. We use it to maintain the app, improve accessibility, and improve the Meet My Menu experience. We may also store or process location information associated with restaurants you search for, scan, or save; this is not continuous tracking and is not used for advertising or behavioral tracking.

3. How We Collect Information

Information may be collected directly when you enter it, scan or upload a menu, speak or type in the app, or save a restaurant; automatically through normal app use and diagnostic logging; from Google if you choose Google Sign-In; and from restaurant menus, restaurant information, or other sources used to provide menu information.

4. How We Use Information

We use information to read and interpret menus; make menus accessible through text and speech; answer menu questions; identify ingredient and allergen information; personalize recommendations; remember saved preferences, restaurants, and menus; sync data when enabled; authenticate users; process voice commands and generate speech; diagnose inaccurate processing and technical problems; maintain security and reliability; understand use; develop and improve features; respond to support or privacy requests; and comply with applicable law. We do not create targeted advertising profiles.

5. AI and Automated Processing

Meet My Menu relies on AI to understand menus and answer questions. Menu photographs and text, questions, transcribed voice requests, and relevant preferences such as entered allergies may be sent to an AI provider when necessary. AI systems can make mistakes. Meet My Menu should not be relied upon as the sole source for medical, allergy, ingredient, or food-safety decisions.

6. Allergen and Ingredient Information

We may highlight ingredients and allergens based on available menu information and your preferences or allergies. Menus may be inaccurate or incomplete, ingredients can change, cross-contamination may occur, AI may misunderstand menus, and Meet My Menu does not independently verify restaurant ingredients. Meet My Menu is not a substitute for speaking directly with restaurant staff about allergies, ingredients, cross-contact, or food preparation. Confirm relevant information with the restaurant before ordering or consuming an item.

7. When We Share Information

Meet My Menu does not sell personal information. We share it only as reasonably necessary to operate the service or as described here.

OpenAI

OpenAI may process menu photos and text, questions and commands, conversation content, and voice input or transcription-related information to analyze menus, understand questions, transcribe voice interactions, and generate responses.

Cartesia

Cartesia may receive response text and related technical information when its voice is used, and converts text into spoken audio.

Google

Google authenticates Google Sign-In. We receive limited sign-in information such as verified name and email, not your password or unrestricted access to your Google account.

Vercel

Vercel hosts parts of the app and backend. Its infrastructure may process or store account/profile information, saved restaurant and menu information, usage and diagnostic logs, and menu photos you have chosen to store.

Service Providers, Legal Reasons, and Transfers

We may use future providers for hosting, infrastructure, security, analytics, support, or other necessary services. They may process information only to provide services to Meet My Menu and are not authorized by us to use it for their advertising. We may disclose information to comply with law or valid legal process, protect rights or safety, or investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents, or agreement violations. Information may be transferred in a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, sale of assets, or similar transaction, subject to applicable law.

8. Advertising and Sale of Personal Information

Meet My Menu does not run third-party ads, use advertising trackers to build profiles, sell personal information to advertisers, or share information with advertisers for targeted advertising.

9. Where Information Is Stored

Meet My Menu uses local device storage and cloud infrastructure. Preferences and saved restaurants may first be stored on your device until you delete them in the app, clear local data, delete the app (depending on device/OS), sign out, or use a feature that clears them. If cloud sync is enabled, profile, preferences, saved restaurants, and associated data may also be stored in cloud infrastructure so they remain available on another device or after reinstallation.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. Data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS or comparable encrypted connections, but no transmission or storage is completely secure. If notification is required after a data-security incident, we will take steps consistent with our legal obligations.

11. How Long We Keep Information

Retention depends on the data and how the app is used. Local data remains until you remove, clear, sign out of, or delete it as applicable. Cloud-synced information may remain while an account is active. On a deletion request, we will delete or de-identify account information unless retention is required or permitted for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, enforcing agreements, required business records, or legal obligations. Usage and diagnostic logs may be retained as reasonably necessary for troubleshooting, security, performance, and improvement. We do not intentionally retain menu photos unless you enable “Save menu photos for analysis.” You may request deletion of stored photos associated with your account. Deleted information may temporarily remain in encrypted backups, system logs, or disaster-recovery systems until overwritten or expired through normal retention processes.

12. Your Privacy Choices

You may use Meet My Menu without Google Sign-In where supported; choose whether to save restaurants; change accessibility and profile preferences; remove restaurants; clear profile information through Settings; keep photo storage off; disable camera or microphone permissions through device settings; and request access, correction, deletion of cloud-stored information, or account deletion. Disabling camera or microphone access may prevent features requiring those permissions.

13. Account and Data Deletion

You may delete locally stored information with available Meet My Menu controls. Where an account is provided, you may request deletion of your account and associated cloud-stored personal information. Account-deletion controls may be available in Settings or the account section. You may also contact contact@meetmymenu.com.
