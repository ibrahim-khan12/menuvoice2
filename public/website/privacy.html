Meet My Menu Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Meet My Menu (“Meet My Menu,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is designed to help blind and visually impaired users independently access restaurant menus. The app can read menus aloud, answer questions about menu items by voice or text, surface ingredient and allergen information, and save restaurants and preferences for later use.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Meet My Menu collects, how that information is used, when it is shared with service providers, how long it is retained, and the choices you have regarding your information.

1. What This Privacy Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • The Meet My Menu web application available at app.meetmymenu.com
  • The Meet My Menu AI iOS application distributed through the Apple App Store
  • Related Meet My Menu services that link to this Privacy Policy

The web and iOS versions provide substantially the same Meet My Menu service and are referred to collectively as the “app” throughout this policy.

This policy explains how Meet My Menu handles information when you use our website and app. Meet My Menu helps diners explore restaurant menus through audio, voice, and screen-reader-friendly tools.

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, dining preferences, allergies, saved restaurants, saved menu information, and messages or voice recordings you submit while using the app.

When you use menu capture, we process the menu photos you select. Menu photos are uploaded only when you turn on the app setting to save menu photos for analysis. We also collect limited technical and usage information, such as device and browser details, session identifiers, screens visited, and feature events.

How we use information

We use information to provide the app, personalize menu guidance, save your settings and restaurants, improve reliability and accessibility, understand how features are used, and protect the service from misuse.

Your allergy and preference information is used to tailor the menu guidance the app provides. It is not a substitute for confirming ingredients or allergens with restaurant staff.

Where information is stored and processed

Your app data is stored on your device first. If you sign in with an email address, your profile and saved restaurants may also sync to Meet My Menu's cloud storage so they can be restored later.

To provide menu analysis, conversation, speech-to-text, and spoken responses, the app may send the content needed for that request, such as menu text or images, conversation text, and audio recordings, to our AI service providers. Meet My Menu currently uses OpenAI and may use Cartesia for voice services when enabled.

Sharing

We share information with service providers only as needed to run the app, including cloud hosting and storage providers and the AI and voice providers described above. We do not sell personal information.

We may also disclose information if required by law or when necessary to protect the safety, rights, or security of Meet My Menu, its users, or the public.

Your choices

You can update or remove saved restaurants and change your preferences in the app. You can choose whether menu photos are saved for analysis. You may also ask us to access, correct, or delete cloud-synced personal information by emailing contact@meetmymenu.com.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. We retain information for as long as it is needed to operate the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Children

Meet My Menu is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the product changes. We will post the updated version here and revise the effective date. Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@meetmymenu.com.

Need help?

For privacy questions or a request about your information, email us.
